How to Play

Welcome to the Procedure Text Racing Challenge! Your goal is to answer English procedure text questions correctly to move your car along the race track and win the race.

  • Start by entering your name, selecting your class, car, and car color.
  • You will receive a question with multiple choice answers.
  • Answer correctly to move your car forward along the track.
  • Three correct answers in a row give you a NITRO BOOST for extra speed.
  • Answer fast to get TIME BONUS points.
  • HOTS questions give DOUBLE POINTS!
  • Beware of challenges in PIT STOP and FINAL LAP.
  • Race until all questions are answered or time runs out.
  • Have fun and race to victory!
